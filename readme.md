## COMP2100 Practice Questions

Students,

This repository contains a selection of programming problems on material related to the course designed to help you prepare for the exam.

1. The `misc-questions` folder contains loose-leaf problems labelled by their topic.

2. The `past-exams` folder contains some past examination papers.

3. It is also recommended that you revise the lab/homework content.

Please refer to the `readme` files in each individual question for problem statements.

While working through these problems, please keep in mind:
- It may take some work to get each problem to work in your IDE. This is due to the format of the exam changing over time.
- The style and content of the problems here do not necessarily reflect either the style or the content of this semester's examination. If you are unsure whether certain content is examinable, ask on the forum.
- Please refer primarily to the exam timetable and forum announcements (which may not be made yet at the time you are reading this) for information regarding the style of the exam.
- We do not provide solutions to any of these problems, but feel free to ask us questions or collaborate with other students. In particular, no software development problem has a single correct solution.
- When a problem tells you to 'upload your solutions to Wattle' or something similar, please ignore this instruction. These are still present since we have not modified the problems from how they were originally posed.
